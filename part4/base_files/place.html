Big Sur, California

Cliffside Cabin

A one-room cabin perched above the Pacific, built from reclaimed redwood and warmed by a wood stove. Fall asleep to the surf, wake up to fog burning off the cliffs. Ten minutes from the nearest trailhead, no cell signal, and a stack of field guides on the shelf.

Two can sleep comfortably; a third can take the window nook with the extra bedding provided. Check-in is self-service with a lockbox code sent the morning of arrival.
