Sandstone Retreat

A stone-and-clay retreat tucked against the sandstone cliffs of AlUla, minutes from the tombs of Hegra and the old mudbrick town. Wake before dawn for the hot-air balloons, spend the heat of the day in the shaded courtyard, and watch the rock glow copper at sunset.

Two can sleep comfortably; a third can take the majlis nook with the extra bedding provided. Check-in is self-service with a lockbox code sent the morning of arrival, and Arabic coffee and dates are waiting on the tray.
